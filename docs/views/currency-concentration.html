Limitations of this view

  • Each share is calculated on 'Value Amount (Display)', the FX-translated amount, because the currency totals in 'Value Amount' are in different units and their sum would not be a meaningful denominator.
  • 27 groups were returned. The 24 largest are shown individually and the remaining 3 are combined into "Other" - a breakdown this long is not readable as a management view. Ask for a specific group, or a top-N, to go further.
  • The amounts are in different currencies and are therefore not directly comparable or summable.
  • The view is scoped to the value date range from 2026-07-20 to 2026-08-18.
  • This is synthetic, non-production data.
